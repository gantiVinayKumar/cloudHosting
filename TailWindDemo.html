Largest Oceans

Pacific Ocean

The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth, covering more than 63 million square miles.

  • Area: 63,800,000 sq mi
  • Deepest Point: Mariana Trench (36,070 ft)
  • Location: Between Asia, Australia & the Americas

Atlantic Ocean

The Atlantic Ocean is the second-largest ocean, known for its S-shaped basin and the historical importance of its trade routes.

  • Area: 41,100,000 sq mi
  • Deepest Point: Puerto Rico Trench (27,493 ft)
  • Location: Between the Americas, Europe & Africa

Indian Ocean

The Indian Ocean is the third-largest ocean, bounded by Asia, Africa, and Australia. It is the warmest ocean on Earth.

  • Area: 27,240,000 sq mi
  • Deepest Point: Java Trench (25,344 ft)
  • Location: Between Africa, Asia & Australia

UY Scuti

UY Scuti is the largest known star by radius and volume. It is a red supergiant located in the constellation Scutum. It is about 1,700 times the radius of our Sun, and approximately 9,500 light-years away. Despite its enormous size, it has a short lifespan.

VY Canis Majoris

VY Canis Majoris is a red supergiant in the constellation Canis Major. It is about 1,420 times the radius of the Sun. Located 3,900 light-years away, it shines incredibly bright, though it is not always visible without a telescope.

Betelgeuse

Betelgeuse is a red supergiant in the constellation Orion. It is about 1,000 times the size of our Sun and located 642 light-years away. The star has a reddish hue due to its low surface temperature.
